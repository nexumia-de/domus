# Culina (Cook's Workbench)

Your daily kitchen companion for cooking better and more varied
([decision](docs/decisions/daily-flow.md)). Open it and get three ideas
for today without typing anything: one that went well before, one new
dish close to what you know, one in season. Tick what you have or what
is in the pan, and Culina says what fits, when to add it, what it still
needs and what dish it can become. Each dish teaches one technique from
sourced notes, and a short verdict after cooking feeds the next ideas.
No inventory to keep: only your staples (spices, flour, sugar …), set up
once. A dish is put together from a building kit (bases such as sauces
and doughs, components that fill a dish's slots, ingredients), and what
you built and cooked is kept in the notebook, with versions and tasting
notes ([decision](docs/decisions/building-kit.md)).

Status: in use, as a web app and a native Linux app. Built: one Cook
screen over own and shipped dishes with a filter and the stock, the
notebook with versions, ratio cards, 135 shipped dishes of which 94 are
bases (67 are the app's own recipes, written by an AI model, read a
second time, cooked by nobody), technique notes, import and export. Thirteen frames (tacos, burrito,
ramen, pizza, risotto, a rice bowl, a soup, plates of several cuisines) are put together from
the app's recipes, some with a choice of filling. Not built yet: the daily flow (today's ideas, "Cooking now", the
learning point, the verdict after cooking, staples set up once) and what a
dish still needs. The plan is in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md).

Its sister app for drinks is [Mixtura](../mixtura); both use the same
general model.
