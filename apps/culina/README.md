# Culina (Cook's Workbench)

Open it and find what to cook. Culina answers two problems: having no
idea what to cook, and not knowing what goes together. A dish is put
together from a building kit (bases such as sauces and doughs,
components that fill a dish's slots, ingredients), narrowed by what is
in the kitchen and what should be used up first. What you built and
cooked is kept in the notebook, with versions and tasting notes; a
recipe is a notebook entry. Sourced notes on techniques let you learn
skills along the way
([decision](docs/decisions/building-kit.md)).

Status: in use, as a web app and a native Linux app. Built: one Cook
screen over own and shipped dishes with a filter and the stock, the
notebook with versions, ratio cards, 68 shipped dishes of which 33 are
bases, technique notes, import and export. Not built yet: frames such
as tacos or pizza with their fillings, and taste profiles of ingredients
that say what a dish still needs. The plan is in
[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md).

Its sister app for drinks is [Mixtura](../mixtura); both use the same
general model.
