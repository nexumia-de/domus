Data built into Cook's Notebook (de.nexumia.Culina)

The app itself is AGPL-3.0-or-later (see the licence file of the module "culina").
The data below is compiled into the app; its sources and licences are named in
Culina's docs (apps/culina/docs/dependencies.md, source-books.md,
decisions/sources-licence.md in https://github.com/suitably/domus).

Ingredient catalogue
  USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (April 2018), https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
  Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
  Refuse percentages: USDA SR28 archive, same licence.
  German names: Wikidata (CC0 1.0 Universal) and the project's own translation
  (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
  Roles of ingredients: the project's own table (AGPL-3.0-or-later).

Classic dishes
  Each dish follows one named source (author, title, year, place); the amounts
  are facts of that source, the method is written in the project's own words.
  The source, its link and its licence where one is stated are shown on the
  dish screen under "Source". The books are the editions named there:
  Henriette Davidis (1904), Pellegrino Artusi (1891), Angel Muro (1894),
  Jules Gouffe (1877), Auguste Escoffier (1907), and one Wikibooks page
  (CC BY-SA 4.0, by the Wikibooks contributors).

Technique, handling and substitute notes
  Written in the project's own words from the textbooks and papers each note
  names as its source; the app shows the sources behind the note. The textbooks
  are open textbooks (mostly CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, some CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC 4.0).
  No text of them is copied.
