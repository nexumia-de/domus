# What this is

The [helper decision](decisions/mentor.md) ships known dishes by
country, each following one named source. Four agents surveyed on
2026-11-18 which sources open in full from the session and where 25
dishes per country stand in them. The lists are a first proposal for
the owner's review. **No ingredient list or amount is recorded here**:
the agents named most ingredients from memory, which does not count;
they are read from the source when a recipe is written.

Status of a dish: **read** (the recipe text was read in the source),
**heading** (only the title was seen), **none** (not in a source that
opened).

# Findings

1. One good public-domain book per country opens in full on
   `archive.org` (only the `/stream/` path; `/download/` is refused).
2. The scans are machine-read text of old print. Recipe numbers and
   amounts must be checked against the page image before use.
3. Old books give old units (Pfund, Loth), amounts for 10 to 18 people
   or for a restaurant, vague amounts, and references to other recipes.
4. **Dishes younger than the books are missing**: carbonara, tiramisù,
   pesto, lasagne, saltimbocca; tortilla de patatas, paella in today's
   sense, gambas al ajillo, sweet torrijas; ratatouille, coq au vin in
   Escoffier. Germany is covered best, Spain worst.
5. Death years marked "not verified" below were not checked at a
   source in this survey.
