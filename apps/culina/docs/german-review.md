# The German of the app, read as a careful German reader

A tester read the built app in German at 360 px, as a careful German
reader, and listed what read wrong, clumsy or English (2026-11-21). This
document records what was changed for each point, and the changes of a
second reading of all shipped content in German.

**Who read it.** Both the follow-up of the tester's list and the second
reading of all 48 dishes, 41 technique notes, 28 handling rules and 9
substitutes were done by a language model (Claude), not by a native
speaker with kitchen training. The project still lacks such a reader;
until one has read the texts, the German is checked for grammar and
plain sense, not for the words a German cook would use.

**What was not changed.** Amounts, the order of steps and what a step
says is done follow the source and stay; only the wording of our own
method text was corrected. Catalogue names (`Speisesalz`,
`schwarzes Pfefferkorn`, `Weizenmehl, weiß, Allzweck, angereichert`) and
unit names (`6 Stk. Ei`) belong to other work and are listed under
[Left to the owner](#left-to-the-owner).

## What the code now does for the language

- **Dates** are shown in the language's form through one helper,
  `formatDate` in `src/shared/locale.ts`: `18.11.2026` in German,
  `18 Nov 2026` in English. It is used for the version line, the
  tasting notes in the history, "Letzter Export", "Exportiert am" and
  the tasting labels of the import summary. The field where a tasting
  date is typed still takes `2026-11-18` (the form a date input reads).
- **Cuisine** has its own adjective form (`browse.cuisines`), read by
  `cuisineLabel` in `src/screens/dish-filter.ts` and by the history's
  diff; the country names stay for the lists by country ("Zurück zu
  Deutschland").
- **Source lines** (`src/screens/classics/cite.ts`, used by the source
  block of a dish and by the technique and handling notes): the
  transcriber's references (`gouffe.txt lines 14702-14761`,
  `; lines 30656–30712`, "(running head garbled in the OCR)") are never
  shown; in German, chapter, section, edition, page, number, division,
  recipe and "(box)" become Kap., Abschn., Aufl., S., Nr., Abt., Rezept
  and (Kasten), and quoted titles get „…“. The files keep the place as
  it was transcribed. A technique note lists each source once: the files
  keep one source entry per sentence it backs, so the same book and
  place stood two or three times in a row.
- **The import result** after a merge was written three times (the
  status line, the toast and the page); the status line is now left
  empty, so the toast (with Undo) and the page say it, as after a
  replace.
- **The focus ring on the heading** after a change of screen: the
  heading takes focus for screen readers, which stays; `:focus-visible`
  cannot tell for a focus by script whether a key or a tap led to it, so
  the shell remembers the last kind of input (a key or a pointer) and
  marks the heading `focus-quiet` after a tap or a click; the ring then
  shows for keyboard use only (`src/shell/shell.ts`,
  `src/styles/shell.css`).
