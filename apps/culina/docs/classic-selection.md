country read candidate recipes in the books of
[classic dishes by country](classic-dishes.md) and chose 12, at least 7
without meat and fish as the source gives them. The tables are the

fish (Germany 8, Italy 9, France 10, Spain 12 of 12).

## Other findings

## Vague or missing amounts

- **Gazpacho**: water, oil and vinegar are "al gusto"; one pepper and two tomatoes in the Andalusian version; "la miga de medio pan"; no count for the Granada version. Serving size not stated.
- **Sopa de ajo frito**: no amount for any item; the source does not mention bread, only "calar la sopa" (pouring over bread is implied, not written).
- **Potaje de espinacas**: chickpea quantity and cooking time not given; "un puñado" of spinach, "una cucharada" of flour, one egg yolk; the source refers to the chickpea cooking without a separate recipe.
- **Calabacines en pisto**: no amounts; the frying fat is not named; the tomato in the book's pisto sentence is not in this recipe.
- **Patatas á lo pobre**: no potato quantity, no fat named, "unas gotas de agua" as the only liquid measure.
- **Ensalada de pimientos**: no amounts at all; "una puntita de ajo".
- **Alioli**: water to thin unstated; the soaking liquid is "agua ó en leche salpimentada"; the oil is counted (six or seven spoons).
- **Tortilla de patatas (Wikibooks)**: oil quantity given for frying (2 cups) with no note on how much remains; salt "to taste".
- **Buñuelos de manzana**: flour, water and spirit for the batter have no amounts ("masa espesa"); the sugar is 30 g.
- **Natillas amarillas**: no vanilla or cinnamon amount; biscuits (bizcochos) with no count.
- **Flan**: the sugar figure in the OCR reads "20<>" and cannot be read with certainty (200 or 250 g); the puff pastry is named but not described; no oven temperature or time.
- **Arroz con leche**: no amount of any item; the source itself calls the dish primitive and says it is a way to use surplus milk.

## Rejected

- Arroz á la valenciana (Muro line 32631): chicken, pork, sausage, eel; meat and fish.
- Arroz blanco (Muro line 32741): lard ("manteca de puerco") in the method; Cuban rice.
- Arroz á la zamorana (Muro line 32711): pork, ham, bacon.
- Pepitoria de gallina (Muro line 19915): chicken.
- Bacalao á la vizcaína (Muro line 26061): salt cod.
- Salmorejo de chochas (Muro line 23383): game birds; no other salmorejo found in the text.
- Gachas manchegas (Muro line 34781): pork fat (papada) and bacon fat.
- Judías á la tío Lucas (Muro line 33063): "una onza de tocino" (bacon).
- Pimientos rellenos (Muro line 32290): filling "un picadillo cualquiera" and "caldo limpio" with no fixed content; doubtful, dropped.
- Huevos rellenos (Muro line 29883): "manteca fresca" with no stated kind; doubtful, dropped.
- Salsa de tomate (Muro line 8319): "grasa — la que sea"; the source names lard as the usual choice in many kitchens.
- Patatas duquesa (Muro line 30625) and Patatas á la Camarlengo (Muro line 30632): fried in lard.
- Torrijas: no sweet recipe in Muro (only "Torrijas de anchoas", line 27057, which is fish); the Wikibooks titles did not open.
- Tortilla con leche (Muro line 30103): vegetarian (eggs, milk, flour; "una jicara de leche por cada par") but not a classic of the same weight as the potato omelette; the egg technique is covered by the tortilla de patatas and calabacines.
- Ajo blanco (Muro line 8218): vegetarian (almonds, garlic, oil, vinegar, bread) but close to gazpacho in technique and use.
- Espinacas con azúcar (Muro line 31663): vegetarian (butter, sugar, milk) but it refers to the Pasta de espinacas recipe for its base; not followed up.
- Migas (Muro line 34431): only the headings were read; not used.
- Paella and gambas al ajillo: not found in Muro; not searched further; both are meat or seafood in their usual form.

- Weights and measures: line 3027–3031, "El cocido ordinario se hace con: 750 gramos de carne, 125 gramos de hueso"; line 8556, the author's note on the butter quantity of his sauces, "por ser la casi equivalencia de dos onzas" (the quantity is set near two onzas); the cuartillo in lines 35342–35346 (natillas) and 37806–37810 (flan); the libra in line 5377 and 4600; the jícara in line 30119 ("una jicara de leche por cada par"); the cucharada in lines 31544–31551 (potaje, "una cucharada de harina") and line 37806–37812 (flan, "una cucharada sopera").
