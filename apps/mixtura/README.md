# Mixtura (Drink Builder)

A workbench for mixed drinks, with and without alcohol. Not a recipe
list: Mixtura treats a drink as a template with role slots, shows its
balance as numbers, keeps your own specs with their versions and tells
you what your bottles allow and which one bottle would unlock the most.

Status: planning. Nothing to install yet. The plan is in
[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md), the reasons in
[docs/existing-tools.md](docs/existing-tools.md) and
[docs/bartender-practice.md](docs/bartender-practice.md).

Mixtura will be a web app that works offline and can be installed on
Android and Linux. Its sister app for cooking is
[Culina](https://github.com/nexumia-de/culina).
